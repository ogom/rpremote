# Structure

[日本語](structure.ja.md)

> **Daisen Kofun** (Oyama Kofun / Daisenryo Kofun / Nintoku-tenno-ryo Kofun) | Daisen-cho, Sakai Ward, Sakai, Osaka

## 1. Overview

Daisen Kofun, located in Daisen-cho, Sakai Ward, Sakai, Osaka, is Japan's largest keyhole-shaped kofun. It is thought to have been constructed around the middle of the fifth century and is designated by the Imperial Household Agency as the mausoleum of Emperor Nintoku, Mozu no Mimihara no Naka no Misasagi.

In 2019, it was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List as a component of the Mozu-Furuichi Kofun Group: Mounded Tombs of Ancient Japan.

| Item | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Shape | Keyhole-shaped kofun |
| Construction period | Around the middle of the fifth century |
| Mound length | Approximately 486 m |
| Mound orientation | Circular section to the north and forepart to the south |
| Mound structure | Three-tier construction |
| Surrounding structure | Three moats and two banks between them |

This reference focuses on the following structural elements:

- Three-tier mound
- Three moats
- Two banks between the moats
- Two round attached kofun constructed on the middle bank

## 2. Mound structure

The mound has a keyhole shape, with the circular section facing north and the forepart facing south. Rectangular projections called tsukuridashi are present on both sides of the narrow junction.

The mound was covered with fukiishi stones and furnished with cylindrical and representational haniwa.

### Dimensions

| Part | Dimension |
| --- | ---: |
| Mound length | Approximately 486 m |
| Circular-section diameter | Approximately 249 m |
| Circular-section height | Approximately 35.8 m |
| Forepart width | Approximately 307 m |
| Forepart height | Approximately 33.9 m |

### Three-tier construction

The mound consists of the following three tiers from bottom to top:

1. **Tier 1 (base):** The widest tier, forming the base of the mound
2. **Tier 2 (middle):** The intermediate tier built above tier 1
3. **Tier 3 (top):** The uppermost tier, forming the top of the mound

## 3. Moats and banks

The mound of Daisen Kofun is surrounded by three moats.

> The moats, not the banks, form the threefold structure. Two banks lie between the three moats.

From the center outward, the structure is as follows:

```text
Mound
  ↓
Inner moat
  ↓
Inner bank
  ↓
Second moat
  ↓
Middle bank
  ↓
Outer moat
  ↓
Outer area
```

| Order | Structure | Type | Description |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Mound | Keyhole-shaped kofun | Approximately 486 m long with three tiers |
| 2 | Inner moat | Moat | Innermost moat, directly surrounding the mound |
| 3 | Inner bank | Bank | Between the inner and second moats |
| 4 | Second moat | Moat | Between the inner and middle banks |
| 5 | Middle bank | Bank | Contains Chayama Kofun and Daianjiyama Kofun on the circular-section side |
| 6 | Outer moat | Moat | Outermost of the three moats |
| 7 | Outer area | Burial precinct | Contains several kofun related to Daisen Kofun |

### Outer moat

The present outer moat was re-excavated during the Meiji era. Daisen Kofun forms an enormous burial precinct with its three moats and measures approximately 840 m overall, including the moats.

## 4. Round attached kofun on the middle bank

The following two round kofun were constructed on the circular-section side of the middle bank:

- Chayama Kofun
- Daianjiyama Kofun

They are not simply round kofun projecting into the outer moat. The middle bank itself widens toward the outer moat to form a platform, and the round kofun were constructed on that widened area.

| Name | Shape | Diameter | Height | Construction | Relationship to the middle bank |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| Chayama Kofun | Round | Approximately 56 m | Approximately 9.3 m | Probably two tiers | Built on the section where the middle bank widens toward the outer moat |
| Daianjiyama Kofun | Round | Approximately 62 m | Approximately 9.7 m | Probably two tiers | Built on the section where the middle bank widens toward the outer moat |

Both have structures integrated with the middle bank of Daisen Kofun.

## 5. Surrounding kofun

In addition to Chayama Kofun and Daianjiyama Kofun on the middle bank, several other kofun surround Daisen Kofun. Representative examples include:

- Gen'emonyama Kofun
- Tatsusayama Kofun
- Kitsuneyama Kofun
- Dokameyama Kofun
- Magodayuyama Kofun

They are thought to have close relationships with Daisen Kofun and to form a burial precinct centered on it. Their specific relationships to Daisen Kofun and classification as baicho subsidiary tombs require archaeological examination, so they should not all be classified uniformly.

## 6. Structural summary

1. The mound is a three-tier keyhole-shaped kofun, with the circular section to the north and the forepart to the south.
2. Three moats surround the mound, with two banks between them.
3. Chayama Kofun and Daianjiyama Kofun are located on the circular-section side of the middle bank.
4. The two round kofun were constructed where the middle bank widens like a platform toward the outer moat.
5. The present outer moat was re-excavated during the Meiji era.
6. Several kofun lie in the outer area, forming a large burial precinct centered on Daisen Kofun.
